# Bribri (bzd) translation notes — Tsuru Ù

This demo store is written in **Bribri**, honoring the origin of the Tsuru name:
*tsuru'* is the Bribri word for **cacao**, Costa Rica's original currency.

Bribri is a living Chibchan language of Talamanca. This translation was drafted
from published linguistic materials (UCR grammars and lexicons) and **needs
review by a native Bribri speaker** — especially the items marked ⚠️ below.
Corrections are very welcome; every UI string is listed here with its intent.

Orthography follows the UCR convention (grave accent = falling tone, acute =
rising tone, underline = nasal vowel).

Costa Rican administrative terms (Provincia, Cantón, Distrito, Barrio) and
proper nouns (WhatsApp, Uber Flash, Correos Costa Rica, Cookies) are kept in
Spanish because the location API returns official Spanish names.

## Identity
